Da Nang and Hoi An in 4 days: a practical 3-night itinerary

For four days and three nights, use Da Nang as your main base, reserve an afternoon and evening for Hoi An, and choose one substantial outing for another day. This is a manageable first-visit plan with space for meals and rest.

Hanoi in 3 days: the Old Quarter, heritage and where to stay

For three days and two nights in Hanoi, keep the focus on the city: a Hoan Kiem introduction, one main heritage or museum visit, and a flexible final outing. An extra distant destination is not necessary for a worthwhile short stay.

Vietnam in 10 days: a north-to-south route with three bases

A ten-day north-to-south route can use Hanoi, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City as three main bases, with two domestic flights if they suit your budget. Choose these regions instead of adding every famous stop. The outline starts on arrival in Vietnam, rather than including the international inbound journey.

Flights or trains in Vietnam: how to choose each leg

Choose a flight when protecting time on a long leg is the priority; consider rail when its timetable, stations and time on board suit your trip. Compare door-to-door time and complete costs rather than flight duration or the first advertised fare.

Japan in 7 days: Tokyo, Kyoto and an optional Osaka day

Use Tokyo and Kyoto as two main bases, adding Osaka as a day outing only when connections suit. Seven days is a compact plan; choose just Tokyo or Kansai if international travel consumes much of your time or you prefer slower days.

Thailand in 7 days: a manageable Bangkok and Chiang Mai route

Split a week between three Bangkok nights and three Chiang Mai nights, with an interregional transfer in the middle. The two cities offer different experiences; avoid adding a beach region if your aim is a manageable pace with fewer hotel changes.
