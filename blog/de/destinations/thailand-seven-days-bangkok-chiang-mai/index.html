Thailand in 7 days: a manageable Bangkok and Chiang Mai route

Split a week between three Bangkok nights and three Chiang Mai nights, with an interregional transfer in the middle. The two cities offer different experiences; avoid adding a beach region if your aim is a manageable pace with fewer hotel changes.

Full planning guide · English

Split a week between three Bangkok nights and three Chiang Mai nights, with an interregional transfer in the middle. The two cities offer different experiences; avoid adding a beach region if your aim is a manageable pace with fewer hotel changes.

Place six nights before buying activities

Arriving on day one and leaving on day seven gives six nights in this example. International flight times may change that allocation. Decide the departure city first: returning to Bangkok adds another connection. Separate tickets may require you to manage connection timing and delay consequences yourself; they are not automatically a protected through-journey.

Group a Bangkok riverside day

Choose a manageable heritage and Chao Phraya area with a meal and rest break. Check opening times, ticket scope, dress rules and the actual pier. Public river transport and sightseeing boats can offer different services and prices. Confirm the service before paying rather than relying on an attractive image or a sales approach at the destination.

Use another Bangkok day for your interests

Choose museums, shopping, walks or food stops that group sensibly rather than doing everything. Include traffic and route changes. Ask about spice level, ingredients, prices and seafood components where dietary restrictions or allergies matter. An area-based outing is easier to shorten when it is hot, wet or the group needs rest.

Confirm the Bangkok–Chiang Mai connection

Compare flights and rail by the operating timetable and total day, not only time on board. Bangkok has multiple airports, so match the code on each ticket with the correct transfer. For an overnight train, check the seat or berth and what happens before your next hotel’s check-in. Keep arrival afternoon flexible for luggage and rest.

Give Chiang Mai a manageable old-city day

Choose a few old-city visits, observing dress and photography rules at religious sites. A cooking class or another operator-confirmed outing can fill part of the second day. Review animal policies when considering animal-related activities; a sanctuary name alone does not establish welfare practices. Confirm the route and operating conditions for any out-of-town excursion.

Check the region and actual dates

A single season slogan does not describe every week in both cities. Use recent forecasts and official notices for outdoor conditions. If air quality matters to your plans, consult current measurements and advice before strenuous activities. Sunny artwork is an illustration rather than forecast data. Keep the option to shorten the route or change activities.

Compare like-for-like bookings

Check hotel position, occupancy, beds and cancellation conditions. Compare a tour’s language, meeting point, duration and exclusions. Partner links open the relevant Trip.com category without preselecting Bangkok or Chiang Mai. Choose the destination and dates and confirm the provider and total price before paying.

Common planning questions

Should I add Phuket or Krabi to seven days?

For a slower trip, keep Bangkok and Chiang Mai. A third region adds transport and reduces the time available in each place.

Can every Bangkok ticket be treated as the same airport?

No. Match each flight’s airport code and arrange the correct transfer.
