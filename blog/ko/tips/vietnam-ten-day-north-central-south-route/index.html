Full planning guide · English

A ten-day north-to-south route can use Hanoi, Da Nang and Ho Chi Minh City as three main bases, with two domestic flights if they suit your budget. Choose these regions instead of adding every famous stop. The outline starts on arrival in Vietnam, rather than including the international inbound journey.

Count the nights before adding attractions

Arriving on day one and departing on day ten gives nine nights in this example: three in each base. Overnight flights and different arrival times can change the actual number. Compare arriving in Hanoi and leaving from Ho Chi Minh City with a return flight to one city; neither is automatically cheaper once additional travel is included.

Let Hanoi be a destination in its own right

Use arrival day for your accommodation area and a short walk, then choose a heritage or museum visit and another city outing. Adding Ninh Binh or Ha Long takes time from this section. If either is a main priority, extend the trip or remove another region rather than merely packing more into every day.

Treat days four and seven as travel days

A short flight still involves packing, checkout, airport access, procedures and another check-in. Avoid inflexible admission times on the other end of the flight. Compare airports, terminals, baggage and change conditions. Separate flight tickets may require collecting and checking luggage again; confirm the actual connection responsibilities and timing with the airlines.

Use one Central Vietnam base

Keep time for Da Nang’s city or coast, then give Hoi An a separate afternoon and evening with return transport agreed. Add Ba Na Hills only by reducing other activities or rest. Changing hotels repeatedly during three nights can consume more time than it saves. Follow short-range forecasts and local notices when deciding on outdoor plans.

Group Ho Chi Minh City visits geographically

Choose a museum, architecture or street cluster for each outing and include breaks. Confirm the current opening times and actual route from your hotel. Cu Chi or a river-region excursion requires its own transport and operator-confirmed duration; it is not part of a short central walk. Keep departure day focused on baggage and onward transport.

Use two regions if three feels rushed

Travelers with children or a preference for slower stays can omit one base. Dividing ten days between Hanoi and Central Vietnam removes one interregional move and creates space for an excursion or rest. The useful itinerary is the one that fits your group’s interests, health, budget and energy.

Compare the whole journey cost

Separate international fares, two domestic legs, nine hotel nights, airport transfers, activities, food and contingency. Compare the same passengers and baggage. Match cancellation terms to uncertain connections, save confirmations offline and check nationality-specific entry requirements through official sources. Partner links do not preselect a flight, destination or hotel.

Common planning questions

Is ten days enough for north, central and south Vietnam?

Three bases are possible if two transfer days fit your pace. Choose two regions if you want slower stays or several side trips.

Should both flights be replaced by overnight trains?

Only after checking train schedules and reconsidering rest. A train night does not automatically provide the same sleep as a hotel night.
