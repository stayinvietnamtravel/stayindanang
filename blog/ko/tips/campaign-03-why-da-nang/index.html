Planning idea · English

Think long sandy beaches, vibrant food, riverside evenings and easy access to Central Vietnam adventures. Da Nang makes an inviting base for a memorable trip. Map beach time, the Han River area and any separate visits to Hoi An or Hue. A hotel name or illustration does not establish walking distance to your stops.
