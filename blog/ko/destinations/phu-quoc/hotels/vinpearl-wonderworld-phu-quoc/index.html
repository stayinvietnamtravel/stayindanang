Before booking

Does the Hot Sellers label guarantee the best price?

No. It is a supplied reference category. Check the final price, occupancy, room and conditions of the actual booking.

What details does a transfer booking need?

The full property name, confirmed address, arrival time, passengers and luggage. A similar hotel name is not a substitute for the correct address.
