Three days in Hue: the Imperial City, Perfume River and a slower pace

Allow three days for Hue’s heritage and food, grouping manageable outings rather than packing several royal tombs and activities into one day.

Open / download HD image · PNG →WebP →

Day one: the Imperial City and an easy afternoon

Allow time for the Imperial City within the actual ticket scope and leave breaks for heat and walking. Follow with a manageable riverside outing or Hue food that suits your diet. Check current admission hours, rules and weather before departure.

Day two: the Perfume River and Thien Mu

Choose suitable transport to Thien Mu Pagoda. For a boat outing, confirm the pier, route, duration and bad-weather policy. Dress appropriately at places of worship and do not assume a boat ticket includes other visits or services.

Day three: one royal tomb or more rest

Choose one out-of-centre visit around your interests instead of rushing between several tombs. Compare transport and ticket requirements, and leave a buffer before a train or flight. Ask about ingredients and prices before ordering Hue dishes or desserts.
