Where to stay in Da Nang: My Khe, the Han River or Non Nuoc?

Pick a neighbourhood for your plans first, then compare specific hotels and recent guest reviews.

My Khe and An Thuong: beach access

The My Khe beachfront and nearby An Thuong area suit visitors who want beach time and places to eat within walking distance. Check the actual walking route to the beach, road crossings and recent reports about noise. A sea-view room and a beachfront property are different booking choices.

Han River: a city base

A stay near the Han River can suit riverside walks, restaurants and city sightseeing. Look at the hotel’s exact side of the river and its distance from your planned stops. Allow transfer time for the beach or trips outside the city.

Non Nuoc: a quieter resort-style stay

The Non Nuoc coastal area near the Marble Mountains is an option for resort stays. Check whether meals, airport transfers and pool access are included, and budget for transport to central Da Nang. Compare room occupancy, children’s policies and cancellation terms rather than relying on promotional photos.

This guide covers real places and general planning advice, not a first-hand review or a live booking offer. Check current entry rules, weather, opening hours, availability and prices with the official source or provider before travelling.
