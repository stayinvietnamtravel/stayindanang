Travel tips

Planning a Vietnam trip: transport, weather and booking tips

Build your itinerary around a region instead of trying to cross the whole country in a few days.

Choose a manageable route

For a short Central Vietnam visit, use Da Nang as a base and allow separate days for Hoi An and Hue. For a northern trip, combine Hanoi with Ninh Binh or Ha Long rather than adding long transfers to every day. Leave a buffer before international flights.

Check entry rules and local conditions

Visa eligibility depends on nationality and travel dates. Use Vietnam Immigration’s official e-visa portal and check passport requirements before purchasing non-refundable tickets. Weather varies by region; check local forecasts and warnings, especially before boat trips or mountain excursions.

Compare the complete booking conditions

Read baggage allowances, cancellation deadlines, transfer arrangements and the final price including taxes. Save your accommodation address and booking reference offline. Use licensed transport and follow local guidance for swimming and outdoor activities.

This guide covers real places and general planning advice, not a first-hand review or a live booking offer. Check current entry rules, weather, opening hours, availability and prices with the official source or provider before travelling.
