# Vietnam campaign posters

30 original ChatGPT-generated 1536 × 1024 PNG posters correspond to campaign days and articles in `data/social_campaign.json`. They are editorial AI illustrations, not verified photographs of a property or an offer. `manifest.json` records topic mapping.

The website uses three responsive WebP derivatives per poster from `scripts/optimize_images.py`; PNG originals are offered as explicit HD downloads. `social/` holds 90 JPEG derivatives exported for X, Facebook and Instagram by `scripts/export_social_campaign.py`. Every social derivative preserves the full poster text. Do not substitute a hotel photograph or claim a room in an illustration belongs to a named property.
