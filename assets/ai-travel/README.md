# Travel artwork library

Original ChatGPT-generated travel illustrations with embedded English typography and the Stay in Da Nang brand. Files are HD PNGs at 1536 × 1024. They are artistic illustrations, not documentary photographs or verified hotel imagery. Download originals from the homepage artwork section; localized captions explain the AI origin in all 13 editions.

- `discover-vietnam.png`: Da Nang, Hoi An and Ba Na Hills; featured cover for the hotels/tours/flights planning article.
- `taste-vietnam.png`: pho, mi Quang and banh mi; featured cover for the regional food guide.
- `manifest.json`: filenames, topics, dimensions, creation date and linked article slugs.

For new images, request a destination/blog topic and exact headline, generate with ChatGPT, and add the original PNG here. Prefer short headlines, safe margins, navy/turquoise/yellow brand colors, a discreet AI-illustration label, and no unverified prices, rankings, amenities or discounts. Add the topic-to-file mapping in `scripts/travel_art.py`, then rebuild and check. No image API key is exposed on the website; these images were created during this task, not automatically generated by browser visitors.
