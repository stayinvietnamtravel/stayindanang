Ideas for your trip

Choose how much time you have, then adjust for the weather and your own pace.

1 day

One day in Da Nang: allow a morning at My Khe if conditions permit, a lunch break and a walk by the Han River.

Read guide →

3 days

Three days: explore Da Nang, give Hoi An its own day, then choose the Marble Mountains or a rest day. Allow transfer time.

Read guide →

5 days

Five days: extend with Hue or Ba Na Hills rather than trying every stop. Consider staying overnight in Hue to reduce same-day travel.
