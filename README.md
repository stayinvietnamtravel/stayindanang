# Stay in Da Nang — Multilingual Vietnam Travel Website

Website: https://stayinvietnamtravel.github.io/stayindanang/

## Install
1. Extract ZIP.
2. Open https://github.com/stayinvietnamtravel/stayindanang
3. Upload the complete site, including `assets/`, language and `blog/` directories, `.nojekyll`, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` into the **root** of the repository.
4. Commit to `main`; enable Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch `main` / (root).
5. Hard refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R.

The website uses shared CSS and JavaScript under `assets/`. Upload the complete repository, including all language and blog directories, rather than only `index.html`. Links use relative paths and work with the GitHub Pages `/stayindanang/` base path.

## Languages and blog

English remains at the root. The site supports 13 language editions: English, Vietnamese (`vi`), simplified Chinese (`zh`), traditional Chinese (`zh-Hant`), Russian (`ru`), Korean (`ko`), Japanese (`ja`), Hindi (`hi`), French (`fr`), German (`de`), Spanish (`es`), Thai (`th`) and Indonesian (`id`). Hindi is one language used in India, not a translation into every Indian language. A compact language picker opens the equivalent article in the selected language. Translations are editorial drafts and may benefit from native-speaker review.

There are eight editorial topics in every language: planning, hotel areas, a Central Vietnam itinerary, heritage destinations, the source-based Da Nang 2026 guide, regional food, coastal experiences and city walks. These produce 104 guide pages. A separate hotel-picks section adds 42 hotels in 13 language editions and 13 indexes, bringing the site to 689 HTML pages. The category buttons filter blog cards by tips, hotels, routes, destinations, food or experiences; all cards remain available without JavaScript.

The blog covers real places and practical planning, with links to Vietnam Tourism, Vietnam Immigration and UNESCO. It does not claim first-hand visits, hotel ratings, live prices or guaranteed tour schedules. Generic landing-page photos remain illustrative. The article text and official reference links should be rechecked when entry rules or destination operations change.

The Da Nang 2026 hotel/tour/flight guide is based on the text of [this @stayindanang X post](https://x.com/stayindanang/status/2089517209634869439) supplied by the owner. The original post could not be fetched in the cloud environment. Its Trip.com link (`https://www.trip.com/t/ntD1RotVvV2`) is used for this guide's booking button, and the original post is cited on all 13 language editions. Promotional claims about ratings, cheap flights and exclusive prices are not treated as verified facts. No publication date is inferred from the X status ID.

Every article and blog card has a locally stored featured illustration. Each topic has its own original SVG cover, shared across translations with localized alternative text and an article caption identifying it as an illustration rather than a destination photograph. The artwork does not require external image services and is regenerated by the site build. The original landing page still uses illustrative remote photos.

## Bookdeal hotel posts

`data/bookdeal.json` records all 49 name/link pairs extracted from the owner's `Bookdeal.xlsx`, including source worksheet, row, and workbook SHA-256. The original workbook is not published. Forty-two entries are included: 23 Top Deals and 19 Hot Sellers. Seven explicitly Hoi An/An Bang entries are retained as excluded metadata. Shilla Monogram Danang is marked as being on the coastal Da Nang–Hoi An corridor so visitors can verify its exact location.

The workbook has no prices, discount percentages, ratings, sales counts, hotel photos or validity dates. Worksheet names are preserved as source categories, not confirmed live offers or popularity rankings. Each hotel page has exactly one Trip.com booking link, preserved byte-for-byte from the spreadsheet. Descriptive promotional suffixes in source names are not treated as verified amenities.

The downloadable ZIP contains 42 Vietnamese social posts, a UTF-8 CSV with 42 short posts (at most 280 characters each), and 42 original text-card PNGs at 1600 × 900. These are typographic designs, not property photographs. No social posts are automatically sent to X or other platforms. The 13 website editions share the Vietnamese artwork, with localized page text and alternative descriptions.

`python3 scripts/build_site.py` rebuilds the hotel pages and deterministic downloadable bundle from the JSON and committed artwork using the standard library. To recreate the artwork itself, run `python3 scripts/deal_posts.py --render-images`; this optional command requires Playwright and Chromium at `/usr/bin/chromium`. The extractor used OpenPyXL only when reading the uploaded workbook; normal site builds do not need it.

## Local development

From the repository root, with Python 3 installed:

```sh
python3 scripts/build_site.py
python3 scripts/check_site.py
python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1
```

No package installation or build service is required for deployment: generated HTML is committed alongside the generator. Edit the appropriate content module in `scripts/` and run the generator to update localized pages, blog pages, cover illustrations and `sitemap.xml`. The English landing page is maintained directly except for blog cards, language navigation and alternate-language metadata, which the generator refreshes. Shared CSS and JavaScript in `assets/` are maintained directly.

## Trip.com banners

Three affiliate banner IDs are included: `S14984461` (900×200), `SB20219796` (300×250), and `DB13678212` (250×250). The supplied Allianceid, SID and trip_sub1 values are preserved. `S14984461` appears once as the prominent hotel-search partner frame on each landing page and blog index. Its actual content and search capabilities are supplied by Trip.com; the site does not implement or promise a separate native search engine. On small screens, this 900-pixel frame scrolls horizontally inside its container to preserve readable controls. The two smaller banners scale to fit. Frames have accessible titles and an affiliate disclosure, and include a direct link if the external content does not load. Their content and language are controlled by Trip.com, not by the site's language selector. Do not apply a restrictive iframe sandbox without checking Trip.com's requirements, as it may break their ads.

All booking CTAs open the general Trip.com affiliate URL. Specific hotel/tour deep links are not configured. Photos load remotely from Unsplash, so internet access is required for them; use appropriately licensed, verified location images before commercial publication.

The website does not offer live pricing, booking forms or direct transactions.
Review GitHub Pages' limitations for primarily commercial sites; Cloudflare Pages is a potential alternative.
